Crafting Backend Solutions

I am a Software Engineer with a passion for building software with Java (aka Spring Boot). I aim to solve real-world problems without compromising on quality and performance. Also, I tend to focus on the solution rather than the technology. Each technology is just a tool, and I love to learn new ones and solve real-world problems with them.

I believe that clean code and best practices are the key to building software that is straightforward to maintain and extend. I am also a fan of TDD, and I try to write tests for every new feature I add. When designing software, I follow OpenAPI specifications to create well-documented APIs.

My favorite thing about software development is the challenges it presents and the opportunity to learn new things every day.
